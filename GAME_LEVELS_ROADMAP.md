# 🗺️ Pixel Kitchen Sim — Official Game & Levels Roadmap (Levels 1 to 10)

## 📌 Executive Summary

**Pixel Kitchen Sim** features **10 handcrafted official levels**, providing a smooth, rewarding difficulty curve inspired by classic arcade cooking games like *Overcooked*. Players begin with foundational kitchen skills (chopping, deep frying, burger assembly) and progress toward managing high-paced, multi-station culinary operations (fryhouse breading, diner melts, grand brunch cafe, artisan pizzeria, and master kitchen banquet service).

---

## 🎮 Level Progression & Unlock Criteria

- **Progression Flow:** Completing Level *N* automatically unlocks Level *N + 1*.
- **Level Editor Unlock Rule:** Completing the first **5 official levels** (Levels 1 through 5) permanently unlocks the **Level Editor** and **Community Levels Hub**.
- **Post-Unlock Gameplay:** Players can immediately jump into Levels 6 through 10 directly from the Level Select screen or via the "Next Level" prompt at the end of Level 5.

## 📜 Detailed Level Breakdown

### Level 1: Fry Cook Training
- **Concept:** Orientation and fundamentals.
- **Recipes:** `french_fries`, `salad`
- **Focus:** Learning player movement, picking up crates, cutting potatoes and vegetables, and transferring items to plates and bowls.

### Level 2: Burger & Fries
- **Concept:** Griddle cooking and multi-item plating.
- **Recipes:** `hamburger`, `french_fries`
- **Focus:** Managing the stove top timer to avoid burnt patties while keeping a steady stream of crispy fries.

### Level 3: Breakfast Shift
- **Concept:** Multi-station breakfast timing.
- **Recipes:** `french_fries`, `blt_sandwich`, `cheese_omelette`
- **Focus:** Toaster management, egg frying, and BLT layering (bacon, lettuce, tomato, toasted bread).

### Level 4: Smoothie Cafe
- **Concept:** Container matching and liquid blending.
- **Recipes:** `fruit_yogurt_bowl`, `smoothie`, `salad`
- **Focus:** Loading 3 distinct ingredients into the blender (banana, strawberry, milk) and pouring into cups, plus assembling yogurt bowls.

### Level 5: Pizza & Diner
- **Concept:** Dough pressing, oven baking, and diner favorites.
- **Recipes:** `pizza_margherita`, `hamburger`, `blt_sandwich`
- **Focus:** Pressing pizza dough into bases, applying tomato sauce and mozzarella, and baking in the pizza oven.
- **Milestone:** Completing Level 5 unlocks the **Level Editor**!

### Level 6: The Crispy Fryhouse
- **Concept:** The art of battering, coating, and deep frying.
- **Recipes:** `onion_rings`, `chicken_tenders_fries`, `french_fries`
- **Prep Chains:**
  - `onion` ➔ Slice at Cutting Board (`onion_rings_raw`) ➔ Coat at Coating Station (`onion_rings_coated`) ➔ Fry in Deep Fryer (`cooked_onion_rings`) ➔ Plate.
  - `raw_chicken` ➔ Slice (`raw_chicken_strips`) ➔ Coat (`coated_chicken_strips`) ➔ Fry (`cooked_chicken_tenders`) + `cooked_fries` ➔ Plate.

### Level 7: Diner & Melt Station
- **Concept:** High-volume sandwich griddles and comfort food.
- **Recipes:** `grilled_cheese_sandwich`, `blt_sandwich`, `hamburger`, `onion_rings`
- **Prep Chains:**
  - `bread_slice` + `cheese_slice` ➔ Assemble raw melt (`grilled_cheese_raw`) ➔ Cook on Stove Top (`grilled_cheese_cooked`) ➔ Plate.
  - Toasted BLT assemblies and burger patty searing on parallel stoves.

### Level 8: Grand Brunch Cafe
- **Concept:** Stand mixer batter preparation, fluffy pancakes, and fresh smoothies.
- **Recipes:** `pancakes`, `cheese_omelette`, `fruit_yogurt_bowl`, `smoothie`
- **Prep Chains:**
  - `pancake_mix` ➔ Mix in Stand Mixer (`pancake_batter`) ➔ Cook on Stove Top (`cooked_pancakes`) + `syrup` ➔ Plate.
  - `egg` ➔ Mix in Stand Mixer (`omelette_mix`) ➔ Cook on Stove Top + `cheese_slice` ➔ Plate.
  - Fresh fruit yogurt bowls and blender fruit smoothies.

### Level 9: Trattoria & Pizzeria
- **Concept:** Mediterranean dining combining Neapolitan pizza, fresh salads, crispy chicken tenders, and fruit smoothies.
- **Recipes:** `pizza_margherita`, `salad`, `chicken_tenders_fries`, `smoothie`
- **Prep Chains:**
  - High-tempo simultaneous execution of cold prep (salads, smoothies), dough stretching / wood-fired oven baking, and breaded poultry frying.

### Level 10: The Grand Master Kitchen
- **Concept:** The ultimate culinary gauntlet.
- **Recipes:** `cheeseburger_combo`, `pizza_margherita`, `pancakes`, `chicken_tenders_fries`
- **Focus:** Fast order rotation across all major kitchen appliances (Pizza Oven, Dough Press, Stand Mixer, Stove Top, Deep Fryers, Cutting Boards). Players must manage prep times and table staging efficiently to achieve 3 stars.

## 🎨 3D Voxel Meshes & Visual Quality

- **Crispy Coatings:** Crumb variations on coated chicken tenders and onion rings.

- **Fluffy Pancakes:** Layered griddle-toasted edges, golden butter pat, and dripping amber maple syrup glaze.
- **Melty Sandwiches:** Diagonal crust cuts with gooey cheese pulls and toothpicked olive garnish.
- **Appliance Polish:** Stone-arch terracotta pizza oven with glowing firewood flame cores.
